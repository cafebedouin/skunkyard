## 4. Design

Give the heartbeat afford check two outputs. The floor's single output (`Upkeep.scala:52-54`) is why a tip payment can be signed and then fail `admit`; `MaxDeferredPerBuild` already bounds that.

Split node-check failures that depend on height from ones that do not. Check refusals are left unremembered so a height race is rebuilt (`UpkeepSource.scala:281-286`). A stable refusal is therefore signed again every block, up to `maxTxs`.

When a later job has more than one input, pass every input's tokens into `accountedCost`. `member` counts tokens on the one box it was given (`Upkeep.scala:73-74`). Heartbeat has one input.
